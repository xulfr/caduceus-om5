## Findings and fixes (2026-11-20, emulator / static only, not flashed)

- **Highlight Tint (and the zone placement of every tint and of Black / White).** OM's R/G/B curve tables are indexed by
  *linear* input: the stock table `0x60533138` gives 2056 of 4095 at index 46 and 3420 at 128. The delta tables were
  indexed the same way with the zones split at index 128 (u = i − 128), i.e. at about 84 % output: highlights covered
  only the top few percent (invisible), midtones peaked in bright highlights, shadows / Blacks spread over almost the
  whole picture. `st_tint` (wb.s) now looks deltas up by the curve's output level, `c'[i] = c[i] + delta[c[i] >> 4]`, so
  the zones split at 50 % output. Same formulas otherwise. `st_tint` moved 8 bytes down to `0x6082976c` (the FF bytes
  after `wb_body`) to fit (148 bytes). Black / White now act on the true dark / bright ends: expect a different feel
  from `0565540a`.
- **Selected-colour swatch.** OM's swatch (colour view label 4, `+808`) has 8 images for the OM-1 / OM-3 wheel (7
  colours `0x1083..0x1089` and "All" `0x1082`, label ids); E148's 12 colours mapped onto 7, so it repeated every second
  colour. `wheel_swatch` (ext9, called from `paint_axis` after `gfx_wheel`) puts a blank image (`0x3e83`) in slots
  0..6 and draws the selected colour's swatch as OM's: frame palette 7, 22 × 22 at (586, 90), fill 20 × 20 in the
  wheel's colour. Pixel-identical position to OM's in OM-Emu. "All" stays OM's.
- **Hint overlap.** `tone_labels` decided Hi/Sh vs Mid from setting 2189 (in OM-Emu 3200 until INFO is first used);
  on the camera "Bl/Wh" was drawn over OM's "Hi/Sh INFO". It now follows what the hint label shows (label 16's
  index at +132: 0 "Mid INFO" → IMG_HINT + "Bl/Wh", 1 "Hi/Sh INFO" → OM's), and `tone_text` follows P+1179.
