## Findings and fixes (2026-11-20, emulator / static only, not flashed)

- **Highlight Tint (and the zone placement of every tint and of Black / White).** OM's R/G/B curve tables are indexed by
  *linear* input: the stock table `0x60533138` gives 2056 of 4095 at index 46 and 3420 at 128. The delta tables were
  indexed the same way with the zones split at index 128 (u = i − 128), i.e. at about 84 % output: highlights covered
  only the top few percent (invisible), midtones peaked in bright highlights, shadows / Blacks spread over almost the
  whole picture. `st_tint` (wb.s) now looks deltas up by the curve's output level, `c'[i] = c[i] + delta[c[i] >> 4]`, so
  the zones split at 50 % output. Same formulas otherwise. `st_tint` moved 8 bytes down to `0x6082976c` (the FF bytes
  after `wb_body`) to fit (148 bytes). Black / White now act on the true dark / bright ends: expect a different feel
  from `0565540a`.
- **Selected-colour swatch.** OM's swatch (colour view label 4, `+808`) has 8 images for the OM-1 / OM-3 wheel (7
  colours `0x1083..0x1089` and "All" `0x1082`, label ids); E148's 12 colours mapped onto 7, so it repeated every second
  colour. `wheel_swatch` (ext9, called from `paint_axis` after `gfx_wheel`) puts a blank image (`0x3e83`) in slots
  0..6 and draws the selected colour's swatch as OM's: frame palette 7, 22 × 22 at (586, 90), fill 20 × 20 in the
  wheel's colour. Pixel-identical position to OM's in OM-Emu. "All" stays OM's.
- **Hint overlap.** `tone_labels` decided Hi/Sh vs Mid from setting 2189 (in OM-Emu 3200 until INFO is first used);
  on the camera "Bl/Wh" was drawn over OM's "Hi/Sh INFO". It now follows what the hint label shows (label 16's
  index at +132: 0 "Mid INFO" → IMG_HINT + "Bl/Wh", 1 "Hi/Sh INFO" → OM's), and `tone_text` follows P+1179.

## Startup comparison and wheel fix (2026-11-20, emulator only)

The original six bytes at `0x603e6a3b` are `af 00 c8 66 5c 38`. They take the stock miss exit at
`0x603e6aa3` when d3 is zero. Otherwise they load a0 from sp+56 and resume at `0x603e6a41`.
The two stock loader tasks submit sections in order from 1 to 10. In particular, the section-8 submitter at
`0x60045a68` waits for bit 7 of `0xa26e2660` before calling `0x60046418` with ID 8. Looking at that task
alone gave the earlier, incorrect “8 before 2” assumption.

Strict rebuilds reproduced all three historical package hashes. OM-Emu was started at the actual gamma hook
with a zero state handle and readiness mask 3 (sections 1 and 2). Both a null and a non-null cache were tested.

| Package | First instruction at the cache test | Sections executed before the stock exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `4f7aeb6c` (`4ce7194`) | Stock compare | 2 |
| `0565540a` (`e351a87`) | Jump to `0x613f2ebc`, before any handle check | 2, 8 |
| `9b14e1ce` (`03cceeb`, unflashed) | Same six bytes as `0565540a` | Same unconditional jump (static comparison) |
| `2b3fd53c` | Jump to `0x605af6ff` | 2 |

On `0565540a` the emulator reached both stock exits because it preloads section 8. The trace demonstrates an
early instruction-fetch dependency. Whether the camera's gamma stage ran in that interval on a failed boot is
unknown. The candidate keeps both stock exits and the readiness and state checks in section 2. The section-8 code accesses
the cache table in P only after all ten ready bits are set and the handle passes validation. The RGB setter also checks readiness,
range, alignment and magic before entering its section-4 tint implementation.

A separate no-half-press GUI trace found the tone-view update executing at `0x60621ee8` in section 3 before
its state check. This path also exists in `4ce7194`. Its gate now shares section 7 with both stock call sites. The trace passes the existing ban
on unarmed execution in section 3. Tone and swatch image setters require an armed state. The candidate keeps
the complete `9b14e1ce` resource component, SHA-256 `72e6ae5568dc123399a5e6011c77ec093b042d7bc07a56fc095afc07b12f3e43`.
The swatch's fourth image and the output-level version of `st_tint` first appeared after the reported loop.

The 1544-byte P fits the stock class-1 allocation (2048 usable bytes). Descriptor storage ends before offset
1504, the generation occupies 1504–1507 and four 8-byte cache entries occupy 1508–1539. The last class-1
block begins at pool base + 20 × 2112. The handle limit now uses that address. The startup test checks the
1544-byte initialization and the 16 guard bytes immediately after it.
