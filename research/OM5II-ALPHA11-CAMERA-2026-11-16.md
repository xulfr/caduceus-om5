# Alpha11 camera report

The owner reported that the OM-5 Mark II (E148, stock 1.1) powered on with alpha11
after the Windows installer’s starter-bank correction. The release manifest gives
package SHA-256 `5384eafef3cb06de4761dba50a09112135fa2cdfacb6716d9e4d0423f5b7072a`.
The owner identified the build through the installer workflow.

- The owner reported that the missing ND32 and ND64 icons in the CP carousel were fixed.
- Opening Monochrome Color Filter no longer restarted the camera. The labels above and
  below the centre area were visible, and turning the dial changed the setting.
- The filter graphic was absent. Alpha11 had restored the stock painter while
  investigating the alpha10 restart.

The report covers the two alpha10 defects and the remaining filter display gap.
