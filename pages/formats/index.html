Experimental firmware. Caduceus is an early, unofficial release for the OM-5 Mark II on firmware 1.1. Installing any modified firmware carries a risk to your camera. Read the safety notes.

File formats

C:\OM5MOD\LOOKS.BIN (OM5F, version 1)

Little-endian. 32 + 80 × n bytes, n = 1..10 (at most 832 bytes).

Offset Size Field
0 4 magic OM5F
4 4 total length
8 4 CRC-32 (zlib) of bytes 12..end
12 2 version 1
14 2 look count n
16 16 zero
32 80 × n looks: a 64-byte record, then a 16-byte name

Record (signed bytes; the offsets are those of the OM-3 project's LOOKS.BIN record):

Offset Field Range
0 Hue, 12 colours −7..7
12 Lightness, 12 colours −7..7
24 Saturation, 12 colours −5..5
36 WB shift on (0: the camera sets the shift to 0) 0 / 1
37, 38 WB A/B, G/M −7..7
41 Contrast −2..2
42 Sharpness −2..2
43 Shading −5..5
44, 45, 46 Shadows, Midtones, Highlights −7..7

Colours run clockwise from Yellow, as on the camera's wheel: Yellow, Orange, Orange-red, Red, Magenta, Violet, Blue, Blue-cyan, Cyan, Green-cyan, Green, Yellow-green. Bytes 39, 40 and 47..63 are not read (write zero). Name: 1–12 printable ASCII characters, zero-padded. @ is not allowed: the camera treats it as a display command. The camera checks the first 12 name bytes and rejects an empty name, @, control characters and non-ASCII bytes. Older saved names containing these bytes display ? in their place; stored settings are not rewritten. LookLab and the Python writer also reject leading and trailing spaces.

The camera checks the length, magic, version, count, CRC and every byte in the table against its range; a file that fails is ignored as a whole. Writers: card/mklooks.py, LookLab (looklab/app.js), byte-identical.

Where a look lives in the camera

A look is written to the slot's own settings (profile p = 0..2): Saturation 928 + 12p + colour, Hue / Lightness in part 1 of the same cells (0x4831 tag, hsl.s), Contrast 908 + p, Sharpness 904 + p, Shading 912 + p, Highlights / Midtones / Shadows 976 + 3p .. 978 + 3p, the WB shift in the current WB mode's shift settings (wb.s then keeps the profile's WB record). Its name and number are in part 1 of four otherwise unused cells: 916–919 (slot 1), 924–927 (slot 2), 972–975 (slot 3): word 0 = 0x464C << 16 | number << 8, words 1–3 = 12 name bytes.
